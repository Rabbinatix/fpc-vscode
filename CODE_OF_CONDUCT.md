# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to work on a language together.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to parsers, to FPC or to open source.
- Argue about ideas, not people. Disagree about the grammar as hard as you like; don't make it personal.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you mean. Questions are welcome; nobody has to know everything.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
  you speak for the project.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close threads, and block people who don't follow this. They use good
judgement and will explain when they can.

**To report a problem,** use the contact form at https://rabbinatix.com (choose "support") and say it is about this
project's conduct, or, if the repository has private reporting turned on, through its Security tab on GitHub. Reports are read by the
maintainers and kept private.
